# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes airline passenger satisfaction data to identify customer
segments with the highest dissatisfaction rates and determine which services
contribute most to negative passenger experiences.

The SMART framework is used to define a measurable business objective, while
descriptive and inferential statistics are applied to validate findings and
support data-driven recommendations.

The analysis covers:

- Passenger satisfaction distribution
- Customer demographic characteristics
- Travel characteristics
- In-flight service evaluation
- Ground and airport service evaluation
- Flight delay impact analysis
- Statistical analysis

---

## 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Performing data exploration and cleaning.
- Identifying missing values and data inconsistencies.
- Understanding passenger demographics and travel behavior.
- Exploring satisfaction and dissatisfaction distributions.

---

## 2. Business Analysis with SMART Method

Defining a structured business objective:

**Objective:**
Reduce passenger dissatisfaction by 1% within 2 months by improving service
quality in the highest dissatisfaction passenger segments and lowest-rated
service categories.

SMART Framework:

- **Specific:** Improve airline service quality to reduce dissatisfaction.
- **Measurable:** Achieve a 1% reduction in overall dissatisfaction rate.
- **Achievable:** Focus on services and customer segments with the highest impact.
- **Relevant:** Improve customer experience and business profitability.
- **Time-Bound:** Within 2 months.

---

## 3. Statistical Analysis

Applying descriptive and inferential statistics to validate business findings.

Analysis includes:

- Descriptive Statistics
- Shapiro-Wilk Normality Test
- Mann-Whitney U Test
- Chi-Square Test of Independence
- Customer Segmentation Analysis
- Dissatisfaction Rate Comparison

---

## 4. Data Visualization

Creating visualizations to understand:

- Satisfaction distribution

- Service performance
- Delay patterns
- Dissatisfaction drivers

# 🔍 Key Findings

### Customer Segments with Highest Dissatisfaction

- Personal Travel Passengers
- Eco Class Passengers
- Passengers aged 7–20 years

### Lowest-Rated Services

- Inflight WiFi Service
- Ease of Online Booking
- Online Boarding

### Delay Analysis

Departure and arrival delays show relatively small differences across passenger
groups, indicating that delays are not the primary driver of dissatisfaction.

### Recommendation

Focus service improvement efforts on:

- Online Booking Experience
- Online Boarding Process
- Inflight WiFi Quality

Priority customer segments:

- Personal Travel Passengers
- Eco Class Passengers
- Young Passengers (7–20 years)
